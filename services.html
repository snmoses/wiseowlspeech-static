Welcome to WiseOwlSpeech!

This is a speech-language pathology private practice located in Calgary, Alberta with in-person services also in Edmonton and Camrose. Telepractice or online services are also available throughout Alberta and the Northwest Territories. We provide quality, individualized speech and language therapy services to children, adults, and their families. We believe in a family/client-centered philosophy that embraces collaboration to meet the needs of you or your loved one. Therapy and assessment services are provided in your home or online!

WiseOwlSpeech offers complete speech and language therapy services to families and individuals. The service provided is individualized, depending on you or your child’s needs. Services are provided in your home or online. Online therapy is convenient, face-to-face, fun and effective!

  • Screenings
  • Comprehensive Evaluations
  • Effective Speech and Language Therapy
  • Do you have concerns about you or your child’s communication skills? Not sure if you need therapy or not?
  • Affordable brief screenings can be conducted to determine if a more extensive evaluation is needed.
  • Screenings can help identify those who are within normal limits of development as well as those who may need more help.
  • Complete assessments of speech and language skills using a variety of evaluation tools can be conducted.
  • A formal assessment report can also be provided, if requested.
  • Family-centered, collaborative therapy in a fun, relaxing environment.
  • Following the child’s lead - what does your child enjoy? What is motivating?
  • Respectful, kind, patient, and nurturing treatment.
  • Responsiveness to you and your child’s needs.
  • How fast is progress made? Progress is impacted by several factors including severity of the disorder, personality, family involvement, home practice, duration/frequency of therapy, client motivation, to name a few.
  • Therapy is provided in 1-hour sessions one to three times per week.
