Sippy Cups Be Gone!

Learn about better options for your child.


March 1, 2017


I just read this great article in The ASHA Leader about sippy cups and their adverse effects on both speech and feeding development. There are many other options available which are more conducive to appropriate oral and facial development. Take a look! Would you consider getting rid of your children's sippy cups? I think I am going to pass this article on to my families so that they can see the benefits of not using sippy cups with their children. Check out the full article here: Sippy Cups: 3 Reasons to Skip Them and What To Offer Instead.
