Happy New Year!

WiseOwlSpeech is now in Calgary, Alberta!

January 1, 2016

Happy New Year from WiseOwlSpeech! As many of you know, 2015 was a year of big change as we moved from Denver, Colorado back to Calgary, Alberta! As well, my WiseOwlSpeech Facebook page reached a new milestone in 2015 with over 200 "Likes"! Having this many readers enjoy and support my page has been amazing and rewarding, and totally unexpected. Thank you so much for your support! (Update on July 22, 2024: WiseOwlSpeech has 791 followers on Facebook!)

As you can see from this picture, my little owl family continues to grow, thanks to my clients and friends. So from all of us, to all of you, best wishes for a fantastic year in 2016!
