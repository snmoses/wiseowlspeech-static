"Smart, caring speech and language pathology services for over 35 years"

WiseOwlSpeech is your source for  speech and language services
in Alberta and the Northwest Territories.
Welcome to WiseOwlSpeech!  This is a speech-language pathology private
practice located in Calgary, Alberta with in-person services also in Edmonton and Camrose. Telepractice or online services are also available
throughout Alberta and the Northwest Territories. We provide quality, individualized
speech and language therapy services to children, adults, and their families. We
believe in a family/client-centered philosophy that embraces collaboration to
meet the needs of you or your loved one. 
Therapy and assessment services are provided in your home or online!
