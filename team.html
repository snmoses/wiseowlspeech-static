Cindy McCallum

My name is Cindy McCallum. I am the founder and managing director of WiseOwlSpeech. I practiced speech-language pathology for over 35 years. After discovering speech-language pathology in high school, I decided it was the perfect career for me! Once I graduated from university with a Master of Science in Speech-Language Pathology, working as a speech-language pathologist became my lifelong passion! I believe that there is nothing more interesting or more rewarding than helping people improve their communication, an essential aspect of being human.

My credentials are as follows:

  • Master of Science in Speech-Language Pathology
  • Certified by Speech and Audiology Canada (SAC)
I recently retired from practicing as a speech-language pathologist, but I kept my practice open with the help of an amazing team of speech-language pathologists! Please take a look at the profiles of our very experienced and accomplished team of speech-language pathologists.

Angela Drozda

My name is Angela Drozda. I’ve been a practicing Speech Language Pathologist in Calgary for 20 years. I have a specific interest in the area of fluency and I have experience and knowledge for assessment and treatment of stuttering and other related fluency disorders. Over the years, I have helped preschoolers, children, teens, and adults understand their stuttering and feel more confident about their speech. I also have experience working with preschool and school age children with language delays, articulation delays, and language based academic challenges. I have seen first hand the positive impact that speech or language support can have on communication and academic success. When I have empowered parents to be the agent of change to help their child say their first word, have helped a child or teen be understood by others, or helped a child read and understand written language, I’m reminded of why I chose Speech Language Pathology as a career. The bottom line is that I love what I do and I love providing treatment that is fun and functional.
My professional qualifications are as follows:

  • Masters of Speech Language Pathology from University of Alberta
  • Registered Member of Alberta College of Speech Language Pathology and Audiology (ACSLPA)
  • Extensive knowledge of stuttering treatment approaches including Lidcombe, fluency shaping, and stuttering modification based therapy.
  • Phonological Awareness and Lively Letters Trained
  • Knowledge of common school based treatment approaches such as Visualizing and Verbalizing (for understanding oral and written language), the Braidy (for narratives), and EET (for describing).
  • Teletherapy
  • Experience working with children with PUF and Mild Moderate Funding.

Anna Kommes

My name is Anna Kommes and I have been working as a registered and certified speech-language pathologist since 2007. The ability to communicate effectively is a critical way of connecting to the world around us so we can start and develop relationships, increase our confidence, and feel personal, academic and professional success. I enjoy working with school-aged and adult clients presenting with speech delays/disorders, oral motor disorders, voice and resonance difficulties, phonological awareness delays, fluency disorders and language delays. I also enjoy working in the area of accent modification with adults. Seeing my clients move towards their goals and feel successful is extremely rewarding and it continues to drive my passion for this career. I am a born and raised Calgarian, and enjoy spending time outdoors, travelling, practicing and teaching yoga, and curling up with a good book. I look forward to connecting with you!
My professional qualifications are:

  • Master of Science in Speech-Language Pathology
  • Certified by Speech-Language and Audiology Canada (SAC)
  • Registered with the Alberta College of Speech-Language Pathologists and Audiologists (ACSLPA)
  • P.R.O.M.P.T. Training- Level 1
  • Lively Letters Training
  • Oral Rest Posture Program (ORPP) Screening and Preliminary Intervention

Erika Phillips

Sneha Chaudhary

Hi! My name is Sneha Chaudhary, M.A.S.L.P. and I am a registered Speech-language pathologist (SLP) in the province of Alberta. I have been working as an SLP for the past 8 years. My experience includes working in Canada and India.
I believe that communication in any form is very important, and it is my passion to help as many individuals as I can to communicate their thoughts and ideas. From the time I have joined this field, it has given me tremendous joy especially when I see a smile on parents' faces as their kid speaks his/ her first word. Having a master’s degree in Audiology allows me to better understand the importance of hearing in communication. It also allows me to understand speech and language difficulties related to hearing disorders.
Since I was born and raised in India, I can speak and understand multiple languages like English, Hindi, Marathi, and Gujarati. My multicultural background allows me to provide therapy according to individual family needs especially here in Canada.
I moved to Canada in 2018 with my husband. We are very delighted by the warmth and politeness that we have experienced here. I am still learning various outdoor activities like hiking and skiing, which are so much fun!! It feels wonderful to be so close to the beautiful mountains and lakes.
I am super excited to continue my passion here in Calgary!
My professional qualifications are as follows:

  • Masters in Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology
  • Registered member of Alberta College for Speech Language Pathology and Audiology (ACSLPA)
  • FSCD Approved Private Provider
  • Experienced working with PUF and MM funding
  • Hanen Certified for It Takes Two to Talk
  • Teletherapy
  • Multilingual (English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarti)

Barbara Finnell

My name is Barbara Finnell. I started my career path in 1989 as a teacher in rural coastal Northern California. When I finished my Master of Arts in Speech and Hearing Sciences, I continued working in the same school district as a speech language pathologist. After living and working there for twelve years I moved to Arizona, and began a different career path working in the home health field with pediatrics. In 2016 I married my husband and moved to Alberta. I continue to work with children in schools and home health. I provide speech and language services to children in person and through teletherapy. Although I have been working for over thirty years, I continue learning and growing in my approach to therapy with my clients. When I am not working and learning, I enjoy spending time with my husband and sightseeing Alberta.
